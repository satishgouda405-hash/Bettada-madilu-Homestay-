About Bettada Madilu

A family home in Mullodi that opens its doors to trekkers and travellers.

Our story

In the lap of the hills

Bettada madilu means the lap of the hills in Kannada, and that is how the house feels: a quiet home in Mullodi with the slopes of Kudremukh all around.

Abhilash and Sharadha Amma run it together as a family home that welcomes guests. Many of our visitors come to climb Kudremukh Peak, so the day here follows the trail: dinner when you arrive, breakfast before the trek, a packed lunch for the hike and snacks when you are back.

We are in Mullodi, near Samse and very close to the Kudremukh Forest Checkpost.
