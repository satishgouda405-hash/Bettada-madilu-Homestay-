Welcome

A home in the hills, run by family

Bettada Madilu is a homestay in Mullodi, near Samse, run by Abhilash and Sharadha Amma. Trekkers, friend groups and families stay with us for clean rooms, hill views and food cooked the Malnad way.

We are very close to the Kudremukh Forest Checkpost, so guests can start the Kudremukh Peak trek early. Arriving late is fine. We arrange jeep pickups at any hour, midnight included.
